Physics Simulation: The "Wrecking Ball" Mechanics

Understanding Rigid Bodies, Springs, and Collisions

1. Rigid Body Physics

The ball/cube are Rigid Bodies. They have mass, position, and orientation.

  • Movement: We use Semi-Implicit Euler Integration. We update velocity first, then position.
  • Rotation: Orientation is stored as a Quaternion to avoid gimbal lock. Spinning is handled by the Inertia Tensor (how hard it is to rotate).
v += (F / m) * dt
pos += v * dt

2. The Spring (Tether)

The bodies are connected by a Spring, acting as the "chain" of the wrecking ball.

  • Hooke's Law: The force depends on how stretched the spring is.
  • Damping: We add friction to the spring so it doesn't oscillate forever.
  • It pulls the bodies together, creating the swinging motion.
F = -k * (dist - restLen)

3. Collision Resolution

When the wrecking ball hits something:

  • Detection: We use the Separating Axis Theorem (SAT) to find if shapes overlap.
  • Response: We apply an Impulse (instant change in velocity) to bounce them apart.
  • Bounciness: Controlled by the coefficient of restitution (c).
